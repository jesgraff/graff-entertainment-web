Privacy Policy – Lær klokken med drager

What information is stored?

The player’s name or nickname, chosen dragon, completed levels, progress and settings are stored locally on the device where the app is used. We do not create an account or database containing children’s information.

Does the app collect information?

The current version does not send player names or game progress to the developer. The app contains no advertising, tracking or analytics tools.

Narration

Narration primarily uses audio files stored locally in the app. If an audio file cannot be played, the app may attempt to use the device’s text-to-speech function. Depending on the device, this fallback may use an online service. The player’s name is not included in the spoken instructions.

Purchases

If the app is purchased through the App Store, Apple handles the purchase under its own terms. The app does not require a separate account and does not contain in-app purchases.

Storage and deletion

The information remains on the individual device until you delete it using Settings → Parents → Delete all player data, or until the app’s local data is removed. This function removes all locally stored player names, profiles and game progress for this app on that device. It does not cancel or delete an App Store purchase.
