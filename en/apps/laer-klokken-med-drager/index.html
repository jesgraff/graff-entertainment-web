About the game

Lær klokken med drager is a children’s learning game focused on telling time. Choose a dragon, move the clock hands and complete eight progressively more advanced levels, from the hour hand to minutes and more complex times. Spoken instructions and hints are in Danish.

Frequently asked questions

What does the child learn?

Children learn to read and set an analogue clock. Eight levels progress from the hands to hours, half hours, quarters and minutes.

Is there English narration?

Not at launch. Spoken instructions and hints are in Danish.

Can my child choose a dragon?

Yes. Choose one of three dragons and give it a name.

Is there a time limit?

No. Each level contains short exercises without time pressure.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Lær klokken med drager is a one-time purchase with no ads, subscriptions or additional in-app purchases.
