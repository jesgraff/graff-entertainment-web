About the game

Find den ene is an observation game where you find the one animal figure that differs from the others. Start with four figures, move on to nine and tackle boards with 16 figures as the levels progress. All 100 levels are included in a single purchase.

◉100 levels included
◇Boards of 4, 9 and 16 figures
▥No timer or ads
♡Pinch to zoom

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap the one animal that differs from all the others.

How many levels are there?

There are 100 levels, all included in the one-time purchase.

How much does Find den ene cost?

Find den ene costs just 8 DKK as a one-time purchase in Denmark. All 100 levels are included, with no ads, subscriptions or additional in-app purchases.

Can I play at my own pace?

Yes. There is no timer. You can pinch to zoom, but every level can also be completed without zooming.

Will there be more levels?

If there is enough demand, we plan to develop additional levels.
