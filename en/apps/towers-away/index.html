About the game

In Towers Away!, your goal is to knock down the red towers without toppling the blue ones. Adjust your cannon's aim, make each shot count and work through 150 levels with varied structures and landscapes.

◉150 levels

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Aim the cannon and knock down the red structures. Keep the blue structures standing.

How many levels are there?

Towers Away! includes 150 levels with increasing variety.

Can I play on iPhone and iPad?

Yes. The game is designed for both iPhone and iPad in landscape mode.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No. The game is planned as a one-time purchase without ads or subscriptions.

Is Towers Away! available?

Yes. Towers Away! is available in the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
