About the game

Sugar Sorter is a colorful sorting game where you move candy balls between jars until each jar contains balls of the same kind. The game features 100 levels of increasing difficulty. You can restart a level, and boosters such as hints, shuffles and an extra jar can be earned or purchased in the game.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Move candy balls between jars until each jar contains matching types.

How many levels are there, and what does it cost?

Sugar Sorter has 100 levels. The first 10 are free. You can unlock the remaining 90 levels with a one-time purchase for €2.99.

Can I undo a move or restart a level?

You cannot undo individual moves, but you can restart the current level at no extra cost.

Are there ads or subscriptions?

No. There are no ads or subscriptions. Full access is a one-time purchase.

How do I get boosters?

Boosters such as hints, shuffles and an extra jar can be earned or purchased in the game.
